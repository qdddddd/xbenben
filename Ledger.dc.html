Industry / mobile · sheet 01

LEDGER

Poker profit tracking for professionals.
Brief
Standalone app, built on the session model from Stacked (sessions, buy-ins, tips, cash-out, bankroll curve) and drawn in the Industry system: square hairline objects, registration marks, Barlow Condensed figures.
Assumptions
  1. Ten seconds at the table. New session opens on the numpad with the last setup pre-loaded; a preset chip starts a session in one tap.
  2. Money is the hero. Figures are set in condensed type at plate scale, tabular, always signed.
  3. Mono by default. The system allows one accent, so wins read steel and losses read ink, separated by sign and arrow. The Signal tweak switches to red/green if you want it.
  4. Cash games only. Each session keeps the currency it was booked in, and the ledger sums one currency at a time — imports from another app in another currency can either switch the ledger or convert on the way in. Tournaments are out of scope here.
Screens
Everything in the frame is live: tabs, filters, pickers, the running timer, re-buys and cash-out all work, and the bankroll recomputes from the log.
